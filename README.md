## Selected Work

**[The Architect](https://intria.app/)** — *live*
Graph-based decision support for supply chains. Models how entities, events and relationships connect, and turns geopolitical, macro and supply-chain events into measurable market signals.
`Ontology` `Forecasting` `Financial Intelligence`

**Seraph**
End-to-end encrypted messaging core for Android, built for minimal metadata and resistance to forensic extraction. Olm double ratchet, hardware-backed key hierarchy, encrypted local state, cryptographic erasure.
`Secure Systems` `Applied Cryptography`

**Self-Testing Signal Engine**
Forecasting engine that fuses many noisy sources, grades its own confidence, and detects when a previously reliable source has drifted.

## Areas

**Ontology & Knowledge Graphs** — entity, event and relationship modeling for domains that must be queried, reasoned over and acted on.

**Financial & Geopolitical Intelligence** — event-to-signal pipelines, market impact analysis, multi-source fusion under drift.

**Language Models** — tokenizer-to-optimizer implementations, FLOP and memory budgeting, scaling-law fits with extrapolation uncertainty, pretraining data filtering and deduplication, SFT and RL post-training.

**GPU Performance & Distributed Training** — Triton kernels (fused softmax, matmul, RMSNorm, FlashAttention-2), roofline analysis, DDP / FSDP2 / tensor parallelism, `torch.compile` graph-break elimination.
